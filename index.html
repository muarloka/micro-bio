SECTION 08 · DATA PROVENANCE

Where each brain comes from

real wiring · published datasets · openly verifiable
C. elegans · 302 neuronsOpenWorm c302
Hermaphrodite connectome from White et al. 1986 and Cook et al. 2019 as shipped in OpenWorm c302: 448 cells, 7,379 connections. Each cell is a leaky integrate-and-fire unit.
Cook et al., Nature 571 (2019) · doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1352-7 · dataset: openworm/c302 (MIT)
Ciona intestinalis larva · 177 neuronseLife 2016
Complete synaptic connectome of the tadpole larva CNS: 177 neurons, 6,618 synapses, 1,772 neuromuscular junctions, 1,206 gap junctions. The second whole-animal connectome ever published and the first of a chordate.
Ryan, Lu & Meinertzhagen, eLife 5:e16962 (2016) · doi:10.7554/eLife.16962 · CC-BY
Platynereis dumerilii larva, 3 days · 966 neuronseLife 2025
Whole-body connectome of the 72-hour larva: ~9,000 cells, of which 966 are neurons across 202 cell types. The only segmented animal with a complete connectome. Code and data on Zenodo.
Verasztó, Jasek et al., eLife (2025), first posted bioRxiv 2020 · doi:10.7554/eLife.97964 · data: zenodo 11811680

SECTION 10 · STACK

Under the hood

full stack · nightly research pass · MIT engine
research/ → BioAgents-compatible API
Every night the organisms' decisions and the structure of their connectomes go to a research agent through its documented interface /api/deep-research with JWT auth. The agent links our spike patterns to published work on these specific circuits and returns a testable hypothesison why an organism behaved as it did. Results land in /api/research.

SECTION 14 · METHOD

What's real here and what isn't

said plainly
  • The connectomes are real. Each organism's wiring was traced by electron microscopy of an actual animal and taken from the published datasets above. Nothing is invented or sampled.
  • The accounts are testnet. $1,000 per organism, funded from the Hyperliquid testnet faucet. The orders are real and settle on chain, the money is not. Mainnet is a one-line change and off by default.
  • The brain is a simulation. Real wiring, invented dynamics. The neurons are leaky integrate-and-fire. This is not a forecast, it is a nervous system with a trading account.
  • Profitability is not demonstrated. The organisms did not learn to trade. We feed price in as sensory stimulus and read the motor output. This is an art-science experiment.
